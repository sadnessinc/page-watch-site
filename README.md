# Page Watch — Website Change Alerts in Telegram

Page Watch monitors readable text on public web pages and sends the exact added and removed text to Telegram.

Use it for:

- company careers and job opening pages;
- competitor pricing pages;
- product restocks and price changes;
- application, registration and deadline pages;
- public terms, policies and supplier notices.

## Try it

- Telegram bot: [@pagmonbot](https://t.me/pagmonbot?start=github)
- Website: [Page Watch](https://sadnessinc.github.io/page-watch-site/)
- Job-page monitor: [Job Page Alerts](https://sadnessinc.github.io/page-watch-site/job-page-alerts/)

One public page is free for 7 days. The paid plan monitors up to 10 pages every 30 minutes for 399 Telegram Stars per 30 days.

## Limits

Page Watch tracks readable public HTTP/HTTPS content. It does not log in, bypass anti-bot systems or monitor private pages. JavaScript-only and strongly protected sites may not work.
