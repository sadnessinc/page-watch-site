Questions, answered.

Does it work with private pages?

No. Page Watch accepts only public HTTP/HTTPS pages and never asks for passwords.

Will it catch every visual change?

It monitors readable text, not pixels. Dynamic, login-only or strongly protected sites may not work.

How do I pay?

Tap Subscribe in the bot and confirm the Telegram Stars invoice. Page Watch collects no card or legal data.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Recurring Star subscriptions are managed in Telegram. Paid access remains active through the current period.
